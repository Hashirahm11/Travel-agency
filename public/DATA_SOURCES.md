## 5. Pricing & Indicative Estimates Methodology

All prices quoted on the website are displayed in US Dollars (USD) as **indicative planning estimates** calculated for luxury and premium travel benchmarks:
- **Baseline assumptions**: 4-star to 5-star hand-picked boutique or luxury hotels, twin-share basis, verified private land transfers, guided cultural experiences, and specified breakfast or meal inclusions.
- **Exchange rates applied**: Benchmarked against State Bank of Pakistan interbank and open market rates as of November 2026.
- **Dynamic variables**: Actual trip costs vary depending on seasonal flight surcharges, peak festival dates (e.g., Eid, Christmas, Cherry Blossom, New Year), currency volatility, and room category choices.
- Quotes provided upon enquiry represent real-time confirmed partner rates.
